# Book #11: Plasma Materials Interactions & Sputtering
## Surface Erosion, Ion Implantation Damage, and the Hidden Service Moat

**Book #11 in the ChipFoundryServices Technical Series**

Plasma-material interactions at the surface boundary determine equipment durability, process stability, and fab productivity. This book covers sputtering yield, ion implantation depth, electrode erosion, and reveals why electrode suppliers and maintenance service providers earn the highest margins in semiconductor manufacturing.
