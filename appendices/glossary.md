# Appendix A: Plasma-Materials Interactions Glossary

- **Antenna Ratio:** Ratio of conductive metal interconnect area to the gate oxide area, governing Fowler-Nordheim charging stress.
- **Chemical Sputtering:** Process where ion bombardment accelerates the desorption of volatile reaction products formed by adsorbed radicals.
- **Coburn-Winters Synergy:** Phenomenon where the combined effect of reactive gas exposure and energetic ion bombardment exceeds the sum of individual rates.
- **Demethylation:** Detrimental loss of hydrophobic methyl ($-\text{CH}_3$) groups from porous low-k dielectrics due to plasma radical attack.
- **Fluorocarbon Passivation:** Steady-state polymer layer deposited from fluorocarbon plasmas that provides etch selectivity between oxide and silicon.
- **Fowler-Nordheim Tunneling:** Quantum mechanical tunneling of electrons through a thin potential barrier under intense electric fields ($>10\text{ MV/cm}$).
- **Micro-Trenching:** Localized trenching at the bottom corners of an etched feature caused by ions deflecting off tapered sidewalls.
- **Physical Sputtering:** Ejection of target surface atoms caused solely by momentum transfer from energetic incident ions.
- **Plasma-Induced Damage (PID):** Degradation of gate oxide reliability, interface quality, or low-k integrity induced by plasma charging, ions, or VUV radiation.
- **Recombination Coefficient ($\gamma$):** Probability that a neutral radical recombines to form a stable molecule upon colliding with a surface.
- **Silylation:** Chemical vapor restoration process using organosilicon precursors to re-methylate damaged low-k dielectric films.
- **Surface Binding Energy ($U_0$):** Energy barrier that a target atom must overcome to escape the material lattice during physical sputtering.
- **Threshold Energy ($E_{\text{th}}$):** Minimum kinetic energy an incident ion must deliver to initiate physical sputtering.
- **Vacuum Ultraviolet (VUV):** High-energy electromagnetic radiation ($\lambda = 60\text{–}150\text{ nm}$) capable of breaking chemical bonds and exciting electron-hole pairs.
